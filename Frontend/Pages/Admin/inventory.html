Forecasts come from the TensorFlow stock model. The grey bar is stock on hand and the black tick the demand forecast for the next 7 days (a tick past the bar means not enough stock), and "Stock lasts" fills up to 30 days, with a mark at 7. "Category estimate" means the part was listed fewer than 28 days ago or has never sold, so its numbers are its category's average (marked *). Edits reach the ML worker within about 10 seconds. "Out" uses available stock (on hand minus units held by unpaid orders).
