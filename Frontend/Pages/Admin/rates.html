Shipping rates

Enter J&T and LBC's rate cards. Checkout picks the smallest weight bracket that fits the parcel in the customer's zone; a courier isn't offered if no bracket is heavy enough. Lalamove is always a live quote.

Zones come from the customer's map pin: Metro Manila, the rest of Luzon, Visayas, Mindanao, and island provinces (Palawan, Romblon, Marinduque, Catanduanes, Masbate, Batanes, Siquijor, Camiguin, Basilan, Sulu, Tawi-Tawi, Dinagat Islands).
