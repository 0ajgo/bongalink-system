Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-16 · Applies to the Bongalink online store and its chat assistant.

Who we are

Bongalink sells computer, laptop and printer parts online. We are the personal information controller for the information you give us through this website. Our data protection officer: [name, email and phone of the data protection officer].

What we collect, and why

InformationWhy we need it
Username, email address and password (stored only as a one-way hash) To create and secure your account, verify your email and send order updates.
Recipient name, mobile number, delivery address, landmark and map pin To compute shipping fees and deliver your orders. Saved addresses are kept only if you save them.
Orders and payments (items, amounts, payment status; not your card or wallet details) To process, ship and keep records of your purchases.
Messages you send to the chat assistant To answer your questions, and so the shop can review questions the assistant couldn't answer.
Login times and consent records To keep accounts secure and to show when you accepted this policy.

We don't sell your information or use it for anything else. Marketing messages are only sent if you tick the optional box at checkout, and you can stop them at any time.

Who we share it with

  • PayMongo processes your payment (your name, email and mobile number, for the receipt).
  • J&T Express, LBC Express or Lalamove receive the recipient's name, phone and address to deliver your parcel.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) receives the map pin's coordinates to find its region; nothing else is sent.
  • Google (Gemini) receives your chat messages to write the assistant's replies.
  • Our email provider delivers verification codes and order emails.

How we protect it

Your email, names, phone numbers, addresses, map pins and chat messages are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) in our database, and passwords are stored only as one-way hashes. Only the shop's admin accounts can see customer details, every change an admin makes to an account is logged, and admin lists hide most of your email address where the full one isn't needed.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while it is active. If you ask us to delete it, we delete it completely if you have no orders. If you have orders, we remove your personal details (name, email, phone, street and saved addresses) and keep only the order amounts, items and city or province, so the shop's sales records stay correct. Unpaid orders expire after 24 hours.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to have it corrected, to object to its processing, to have it erased or blocked, to data portability, and to claim damages. You can update your email, username and saved addresses yourself in your profile. For anything else, message the shop through the chat or contact our data protection officer above. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your information, we'll update this page and its version, and ask you to accept it again where the law requires.
